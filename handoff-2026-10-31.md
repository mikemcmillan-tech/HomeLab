## What the video says employers want (2026)

1. **End-to-end automation:** infrastructure as code (Terraform) + CI/CD pipelines (GitHub Actions). Clicking through a console isn't enough.
2. **Architecture + communication:** read an architecture diagram, explain what's wrong with it and why. Being able to talk to stakeholders gets paid more.
3. **Security is the AI angle:** IAM and secure, automated deployment of AI tools, rather than building AI.
4. **Certs get you in the door; interviews test hands-on skill.** Underrated: GitHub Actions, Terraform Associate, AWS SysOps, Azure AZ-104 / AZ-305 / DevOps Expert. Overrated for hands-on roles: AWS Solutions Architect Associate, AWS professional-level certs, CISSP.
5. **Career habits:** interview regularly (it finds gaps, keeps the skill sharp, and shows what I'm worth), track what recruiters ask for, and consider changing jobs after about 2 years.
